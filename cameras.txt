Live RGB-D viewer.
Click the window so keys reach it:
  Q or Esc    quit, including while cameras are still starting
  F           toggle fullscreen (the window opens fullscreen)
  close the window, or Ctrl-C, also quits
Each tile is one camera: RGB on the left, depth aligned to colour on the right.
Depth: near = red, far = purple, no return = black. Scale is 0-2 m (--depth-max).
Status line: fps, frame number, age of the newest frame, percent of pixels with depth.
STALE means that frame is older than 1 s. OFFLINE means the camera thread stopped.
Starting cameras can take tens of seconds (USB reset and white balance). Still waiting after 90 s: use a USB 3 port, then --reset or --fps 15.
