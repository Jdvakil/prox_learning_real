ACT / DETR model source provenance

The model implementation was copied from the local Tony Z. Zhao ACT checkout
(detr/models/{detr_vae,transformer,backbone,position_encoding}.py), including its
Franka state/action dimension extensions. Upstream: https://github.com/tonyzhaozh/act
The upstream MIT license is preserved in LICENSE. Original Facebook copyright
headers on the DETR-derived files are retained.

Local changes: use a minimal local tensor container instead of DETR training and
distributed utilities; omit optional IPython imports; build through a Namespace
without training CLI arguments or optimizers; initialize ResNet with weights=None
because all weights are supplied by the packaged checkpoint. The checkpoint
parameter names and model computations are preserved.
